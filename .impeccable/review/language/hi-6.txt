On Android, Arogio can read steps, sleep, heart rate, weight, distance, active and total energy, exercise duration, floors climbed and water intake from Health Connect after you choose data types and grant access. You select one installed Health Connect-compatible source app at a time. Only that app supplies analytics, and changing the source clears the previous overview and cache. Compatible app names are discovered locally and not uploaded. Readings are used for the on-device overview and held in memory during the signed-in app session. Automatic refresh runs every five minutes while the app is active and when you return. Optional background refresh uses Android scheduling (at least 15 minutes, possibly later) and keeps a recent overview encrypted on the device. Connection preferences are saved on the device. They are not uploaded to the Arogio API or written back to Health Connect. Disconnect clears the displayed and cached readings and stops refreshes. Signing out clears cached readings and stops background work. You can revoke permissions in Android Health Connect settings.
